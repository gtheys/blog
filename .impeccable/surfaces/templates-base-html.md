## Direction contract

THESIS: The terminal is home turf — the page speaks in wide, dark, confident
surfaces where screenshots and illustrations carry the proof, not stock-icon
cards. Refuses the narrow single-column blog-default and the same-size
card grid.

OWN-WORLD: Deep ink ground, teal/green accent pair, amber reserved for the
blinking cursor and one primary CTA. Pill badges with accent borders. Images in
rounded, bordered frames like terminal windows. Blog stylesheet is the shared
source of truth; landing hotlinks it.

STORY: Visitor lands on monetize-oss.com, sees Geert, the why in his own words,
and three proof blocks (interviews, scorecards, blog) each led by a real image;
leaves via a CTA to blog or scorecards. Blog readers get wider measure, image
thumbnails in the index, color in tags and selection.

FIRST VIEWPORT (landing): nav top; hero left-aligned — round avatar, name,
pill row (Open Source · AI · Interviews · Scorecards), display-size "Monetize
OSS_" with blinking amber underscore, tagline, two CTAs. Below: why-statement
at display scale, then split image cards.

FORM: User-pinned reference (rogerio-style wide dark layout) translated into the
existing teal/green world. No roll — brief-pinned direction.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the
finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its
provenance.
